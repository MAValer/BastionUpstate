SERVICES

The four things I check on every visit

Not a menu of upsells — this is the actual checklist, every time, whether you're a five-table restaurant or a two-desk office.

Guest Network Separation

SSID split

Your customers' phones get their own network with their own password, walled off from your point-of-sale system, printers, and office machines. If a customer's phone has something ugly on it, it never gets near your files.

Wi-Fi Security Hardening

WPA3-Personal / WPA2-AES

Most routers ship on WPA2 with a password taped to the bottom of the box. I move you to WPA3 where your hardware supports it, turn off WPS, and retire any password that's been photographed by half your regulars.

Password & Access Cleanup

Admin + device audit

Old employee logins, a default router-admin password nobody ever changed, one tablet nobody remembers connecting — I find what still has access to your network and decide with you what should keep it.

Firmware Check

Update + basic firewall review

Routers get security patches like anything else — most just never get installed. I update firmware, check for known issues on your specific hardware, and set firewall rules built for a shop, not a server room.
